# Failure Scenarios

## Missing Critical Inputs

Ask up to 5 questions. Prioritize:

1. full address and ZIP
2. beds/baths/sqft
3. year built and lot size
4. pool/garage/condition/improvements
5. comp date range and report objective

## Too Few Sold Comps

Actions:

- expand date range only after disclosing the change
- expand radius only after disclosing the change
- consider similar subdivisions or competing neighborhoods
- reduce confidence
- increase reliance on active/pending data and historical appreciation if credible

## Conflicting Public Data

Actions:

- show the conflict
- prefer county records for legal/property facts
- prefer MLS/user-provided data for listing status and DOM when available
- keep public portal values as secondary unless corroborated

## No Reliable Last Sale

Actions:

- mark historical appreciation method as unavailable or low confidence
- do not invent prior sale date or price
- reconcile primarily from CMA and active/pending market data

## Major Renovations Unknown

Actions:

- ask for improvement details if material
- lower confidence
- avoid giving full renovation credit without evidence

## Volatile Market

Actions:

- prioritize recent sold, pending, active price reductions, and DOM
- reduce weight on older comps
- widen value range
- explain pricing risk

## Legal/Appraisal Boundary

Never claim the report is:

- an appraisal
- a tax assessment
- a guarantee of market value
- legal, financial, or investment advice
