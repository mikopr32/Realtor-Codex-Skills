# Document and Activation

## DOCX standard

- Clear title block with guide, agent, market and current date.
- One H1; H2 sections; H3 only when necessary.
- Short paragraphs and useful bullets.
- Simple bordered tables for comparisons, costs and timelines.
- Phone-readable spacing and hierarchy.
- Footer with agent, contact placeholder when needed and page number.
- Sources and disclaimers at the end.
- No logo or branding asset unless supplied or authorized.
- Verify the file opens and contains the complete guide.

Suggested length:

- quick guide: 900–1,400 words;
- standard guide: 1,400–2,200 words;
- research dossier: scope-based, with executive summary.

Usefulness outranks an arbitrary word count.

## Design brief

Include:

- audience and emotional job;
- page count estimate;
- page-by-page map;
- brand palette and typography;
- image direction;
- chart/table direction;
- callout style;
- mobile readability;
- prohibited imagery or claims;
- export requirements.

Keep the brief platform-neutral.

## Activation pack

Provide:

- asset name and filename;
- one-sentence value proposition;
- landing headline and subheadline;
- 3–5 benefit bullets;
- form fields and consent dependency;
- CTA button;
- thank-you headline and delivery instruction;
- email subject, preview and body;
- optional SMS with sender identification and opt-out dependency;
- one organic caption;
- next conversion event;
- tracking event and KPI.

Do not claim the funnel is launch-ready when privacy, consent, delivery link or material facts remain pending.
