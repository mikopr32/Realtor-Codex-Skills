# Research and Sources

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:

1. county, city, state and federal sources;
2. school district and transportation authorities;
3. builder, HOA and community documents for their own current facts;
4. MLS or brokerage data when authorized;
5. reputable market portals with retrieval date;
6. user-provided local expertise, attributed as such.

Cross-check material claims when sources can conflict.

## Time-sensitive fields

Date and source:

- price and inventory;
- rates and payments;
- incentives;
- taxes and assessments;
- HOA/CDD;
- schools and boundaries;
- zoning and rental restrictions;
- programs and eligibility;
- commute time;
- market metrics.

Do not infer missing values from nearby properties or older documents.

## Citation format

Use concise endnotes or a source table:

| Claim | Source | As of | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Link to the supporting page, not a search-results page.

## Conflicts

When sources disagree:

- state the conflict;
- prefer the authoritative and newer source;
- avoid presenting a single definitive number;
- flag the item for user verification when material.
