# Guide Blueprints

## Buyer

Cover:

- local buying context;
- realistic cost categories;
- financing and professional-team overview;
- process and timeline;
- property and community evaluation;
- offer and inspection decisions;
- common mistakes;
- next-step checklist.

## Seller

Cover:

- current local selling context;
- pricing evidence and uncertainty;
- preparation and repair choices;
- marketing and showing plan;
- offer comparison beyond price;
- timeline and transaction milestones;
- net-proceeds inputs without inventing estimates;
- consultation checklist.

## Relocation

Cover:

- area orientation and map logic;
- commute and transportation sources;
- housing types and verified price context;
- taxes, HOA/CDD and recurring-cost categories;
- utilities and official resources;
- lifestyle and amenities;
- moving timeline;
- remote-buying process and consultation.

Avoid demographic descriptions or declaring where someone should live.

## Neighborhood or community

Cover:

- location and boundaries;
- housing and architecture;
- verified price/inventory snapshot;
- amenities and nearby services;
- HOA/CDD and restrictions when verified;
- assigned-school verification resources;
- commute methodology;
- buyer-fit decision criteria stated as needs, not demographics;
- alternatives and trade-offs.

## New construction

Cover:

- community and builder facts;
- models and verified price ranges;
- inventory and incentives with date;
- included features versus upgrades;
- HOA/CDD, taxes and estimated recurring costs;
- warranties and construction milestones;
- builder-representative versus buyer-agent roles without unsupported legal claims;
- comparison and tour checklist.

## Equity or seller report companion

Cover:

- what equity means;
- difference between automated estimate, CMA and appraisal;
- factors that change marketability and net proceeds;
- verified local market context;
- options: sell, hold, rent, refinance discussion with appropriate professionals;
- consultation checklist.

## Property list

Cover:

- inclusion criteria and date;
- verified property snapshot;
- comparison table;
- recurring-cost fields;
- status and availability warning;
- next-step tour or consultation.

Never imply the list is complete or current beyond its stated retrieval date.

## Investor

Cover:

- strategy and criteria;
- acquisition assumptions;
- income and expense categories;
- financing and reserve considerations;
- due diligence;
- local restrictions and source links;
- scenario table without guaranteed returns;
- professional-review checklist.
