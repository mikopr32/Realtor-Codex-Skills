# Quality and Compliance

## Score

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Usefulness and completeness | 20 |
| Local specificity | 15 |
| Factual integrity and sources | 20 |
| Audience and offer fit | 10 |
| Readability and document quality | 10 |
| Fair Housing and professional scope | 10 |
| CTA and activation readiness | 10 |
| Brand and language accuracy | 5 |

Minimum: 90/100.

## Critical failures

- invented local fact or statistic;
- steering or protected-class language;
- outdated time-sensitive claim presented as current;
- mixed or wrong brand;
- missing source for a material researched claim;
- document not generated or incomplete;
- CTA promises an unavailable deliverable;
- bilingual request delivered as a partial translation.

## Final checks

- Every title matches the actual content.
- The guide is useful without hiring the agent.
- Universal advice is not disguised as local expertise.
- Schools are handled through neutral official resources.
- All calculations show assumptions.
- Contact and brokerage fields are confirmed or visibly pending.
- The CTA has one next action.
- The delivery link and filename are usable.
- Source dates are visible.
