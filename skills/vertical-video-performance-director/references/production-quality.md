# Production quality reference

## Hook contract

The first line creates a contract with the viewer. The body must deliver the promised answer with equal or greater specificity. Reject hooks that only intensify emotion without adding a concrete payoff.

## Accessibility

- Make the message understandable without sound.
- Use high-contrast captions and short semantic blocks.
- Keep critical text away from interface overlays and screen edges.
- Correct names, locations, numbers and industry terms manually.
- Avoid rapid flashing or unnecessary motion.

## Rights and permissions

- Prefer original footage, licensed libraries, authorized listing assets or generated visuals with appropriate rights.
- Treat screenshots, news footage, maps, music, logos and listing photos as protected unless permission or applicable use is established.
- Platform availability does not automatically grant commercial-use rights.

## Claims

Record source, publication date, effective period, geography and assumptions for every material financial or market claim. Replace unsupported precision with an honest range or remove it.

## Production review

Check audio before image polish. A clean voice, readable captions, credible delivery and a clear idea usually matter more than elaborate transitions. Pattern interrupts must support comprehension rather than create visual noise.

## Measurement

Compare performance against the account's own recent median for similar duration and topic. Diagnose the funnel:

- Weak initial hold: hook or first frame.
- Strong hold, weak completion: pacing, structure or delayed payoff.
- Strong watch time, weak action: CTA or offer mismatch.
- Saves without leads: useful content with insufficient conversion path.
