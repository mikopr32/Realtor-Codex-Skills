# Underwriting methodology

## Evidence states

- Verified fact: directly supported by a dated source.
- Derived metric: formula applied to verified or labeled inputs.
- Inference: interpretation supported by observations.
- Assumption: editable placeholder used because evidence is absent.
- Unknown: material fact not established.

## Comparable roles

| Status | Use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Sold | Market value evidence | Adjust for time, condition and concessions |
| Active | Substitution and competition | Asking price is not value evidence |
| Pending | Demand signal | Contract price may be unknown |
| Expired/withdrawn | Resistance signal | Withdrawal may be unrelated to price |

Use qualitative bracketing when market-derived adjustments are unavailable. Widen the range instead of inventing precise adjustments.

## Financial definitions

- NOI excludes debt service, depreciation and income taxes.
- Cap rate = annual NOI / acquisition price or current value.
- DSCR = annual NOI / annual debt service.
- Cash-on-cash = annual pre-tax cash flow / cash invested.
- Flip profit = sale proceeds less acquisition, rehab, financing, holding and selling costs.
- MAO is the maximum price satisfying the stated return and risk constraints.

Do not count mortgage escrow separately if taxes or insurance are already included. Do not treat mortgage principal as an operating expense.

## Scenario discipline

- Conservative: lower revenue/ARV, higher time and cost.
- Base: best-supported inputs.
- Optimistic: plausible but not decision-controlling.

Include sensitivity to purchase price, rehab, interest rate, time, rent/ARV and vacancy. A deal should not be called validated solely because the optimistic scenario works.

## Zoning verification

Check official zoning map, code, future land use, overlays, parcel dimensions, setbacks, density, lot coverage, parking, access, utilities, flood/wetlands, deed restrictions and HOA. Distinguish by-right, conditional, variance-dependent and prohibited uses.

## Confidence

- High: primary/current sources, strong comps and verified costs.
- Medium: adequate data with some unresolved private inputs.
- Low: portal-only history, weak comps, estimated rehab/rent or speculative zoning.
