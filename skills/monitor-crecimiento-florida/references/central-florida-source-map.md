# Central Florida source map

Verify current URLs during research. Prefer the most specific project-level source.

## Primary and official

- FDOT District Five project pages, work programs, studies and public-hearing records.
- Central Florida Expressway Authority project pages, board materials, capital plans and procurement records.
- SunRail, LYNX and applicable transit-agency plans, board materials and service notices.
- County and city planning, zoning, development review, comprehensive plans, commission agendas, permits, capital-improvement plans and GIS/project portals.
- Florida Legislature, adopted budgets, state agencies and water-management districts when applicable.
- Airport authorities, utility authorities and public institutions for their own projects.
- Developer, builder, employer, retailer or attraction filings and direct announcements only for claims about their own activity.

## Secondary journalism

- Established local newsrooms and business/trade reporting, including Orlando Business Journal and GrowthSpotter when accessible.
- Local television/news sites such as ClickOrlando, WFTV and comparable outlets for chronology and stakeholder context.

Do not label secondary journalism official. For paywalled reporting, do not infer details from snippets; trace material claims to public filings or accessible sources.

## Discovery only

- Public social posts, forums, newsletters, search snippets and community discussion.
- Real-estate marketing pages without underlying filings.

Use these to find candidate projects, then verify independently.

## Status evidence hierarchy

1. Adopted action, executed funding, issued permit or official construction notice.
2. Official agenda/minutes, application record, study or project page.
3. Direct applicant/developer announcement.
4. Reputable journalism citing identifiable records or officials.
5. Marketing, social discussion or rumor.

Lower levels cannot silently override higher levels. Record conflict and consulted date.

## Geographic discipline

Capture parcel/address when available, jurisdiction, county, city/postal city, corridor, interchange/transit station and relevant phase. Do not assign a countywide impact to a parcel-scale project without an explicit mechanism.
