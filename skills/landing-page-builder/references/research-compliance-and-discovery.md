# Research, Compliance, and Discovery

## Research modes

### None

Use for stable copy edits with sufficient facts.

### Fact verification

Use for prices, incentives, inventory, rates, events, local data, product details and credentials.

### Search discovery

Use for search-first or hybrid pages. Identify primary intent, related questions, credible sources and entity consistency. Do not force exact passage lengths or FAQ schema.

### Competitive audit

Use when requested. Compare offer, structure, proof, friction and destination. Extract patterns, never copy language or design.

### Technical/current

Use official documentation for schema, analytics, privacy, platform and builder requirements that may change.

## SEO/AEO decision

For search-first or hybrid pages, consider:

- descriptive title and H1;
- direct answer near the top;
- useful H2 questions;
- author/business identity;
- internal links;
- updated date when content is time-sensitive;
- cited primary sources;
- appropriate structured data supported by the actual content.

Do not promise AI citation or ranking. Do not add schema unsupported by visible content.

## Compliance

Create a requirement table:

| Item | Layer | Status | Source/owner |
|---|---|---|---|

Layers: law, platform, brokerage/company, professional review, best practice.

Flag:

- protected-class or steering language;
- unsupported proof or testimonial use;
- property/listing data authority;
- rates, payments, savings or income claims;
- privacy and consent;
- misleading scarcity;
- unclear pricing or refund terms;
- regulated health, finance, legal or tax claims.
