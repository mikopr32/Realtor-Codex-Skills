# Quality and Testing

## Score

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Message match and clarity | 15 |
| Offer and audience fit | 15 |
| Page architecture and copy | 15 |
| Form, consent and thank-you | 15 |
| Proof and factual integrity | 10 |
| Mobile, accessibility and performance | 10 |
| Tracking and integrations | 10 |
| Compliance and risk | 10 |

Minimum: 90/100.

## Critical failures

- primary conversion unclear;
- fabricated fact, proof or scarcity;
- form cannot deliver the promise;
- privacy or material consent dependency omitted;
- thank-you state missing;
- integration or tracking described as working without a test;
- wrong brand or language;
- current regulated claim left unverified;
- build reported as deployed when only copy was produced.

## Launch checklist

- Hero communicates audience, offer and action.
- Upstream message matches the page.
- CTA is consistent.
- Form labels and errors are accessible.
- Mobile layout is verified.
- Page weight and major assets are reasonable.
- Privacy and required disclosures are linked.
- Thank-you state works.
- CRM mapping and notifications work.
- Tracking events fire once.
- Time-sensitive facts show a date.
- Owner and response SLA are assigned.

## Test plan

Start with one bottleneck and one hypothesis. Test concept or offer before minor button language. Track a primary business or funnel KPI plus guardrails for quality, complaints and technical errors.

For low volume, use directional evidence and qualitative feedback rather than claiming statistical significance.
