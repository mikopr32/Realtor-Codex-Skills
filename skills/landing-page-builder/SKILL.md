# Landing Page Builder

Own the complete conversion experience from message match through form success, thank-you state, tracking and implementation. Build when an authorized page capability is available; otherwise deliver an execution-ready specification.

## Select mode

1. **Create:** design a new page.
2. **Audit:** diagnose an existing URL, screenshot or draft.
3. **Optimize:** use performance data to improve the bottleneck.
4. **Build:** create or edit the page in an authorized tool.
5. **Build brief:** prepare exact instructions for a named builder.

Do not describe copy-only work as a deployed page.

## Establish context

Determine from conversation, personal context and relevant user-provided resources:

- business track and brand;
- language;
- page type and primary conversion;
- audience and traffic temperature;
- traffic source and upstream message;
- offer and proof;
- geography and jurisdiction;
- platform, integrations and current assets;
- privacy, consent and tracking requirements.

If language is not explicit and cannot be safely inferred, ask:

1. Español
2. English
3. Bilingüe, as separate page versions

Ask one grouped intake only when critical information is missing. Skip known fields. Never invent proof, URLs, credentials, inventory, pricing, incentives, events, rates or results.

## Classify page strategy

Choose:

- **Conversion-first:** campaign traffic; minimize distraction.
- **Search-first:** durable organic discovery and complete answer.
- **Hybrid:** campaign conversion plus useful indexable content.

Do not add SEO/AEO sections to a conversion-first page without a reason. Do not remove necessary context from a search-first page merely to shorten it.

Read [references/page-blueprints.md](references/page-blueprints.md) and select the closest blueprint. State page type, funnel stage, primary action and success event.

## Research only when material

Read [references/research-compliance-and-discovery.md](references/research-compliance-and-discovery.md).

Research when:

- current local or market facts support the offer;
- platform, legal or technical requirements may have changed;
- the user requests competitor or benchmark analysis;
- existing-page performance needs diagnosis;
- search intent matters to the selected strategy.

Skip broad trend research for stable copy edits. Prefer primary sources and current official documentation. Date time-sensitive claims and distinguish verified facts, user claims, assumptions and pending items.

## Diagnose before rewriting

For audits, evaluate:

- message match;
- clarity above the fold;
- offer and value exchange;
- proof and objection handling;
- CTA and form friction;
- mobile hierarchy;
- accessibility and performance;
- privacy and consent;
- thank-you experience;
- tracking and integrations;
- factual and compliance risk.

Use ✅ working, ⚠️ underperforming, ❌ missing, ❓ unmeasured and ⛔ unavailable. Rank the three highest-leverage fixes before producing replacement copy.

## Compare approaches

When architecture is not obvious, present two or three page approaches with trade-offs in conversion potential, speed, build cost, search value, data collection, compliance and operational load. Recommend one.

## Build the conversion experience

Create:

1. primary promise and message match;
2. two to three genuinely different headline concepts;
3. mobile-first section sequence;
4. complete copy;
5. visual hierarchy and asset direction;
6. form fields, field logic and consent placement;
7. CTA states and validation/error messages;
8. thank-you or delivery state;
9. CRM/source mapping and notifications;
10. tracking events and UTMs;
11. privacy, disclosure and pending requirements;
12. test hypothesis and KPIs.

Use one primary conversion per page. Secondary links may exist for legal, accessibility, navigation required by the platform, or a lower-intent fallback when strategically justified.

Read [references/forms-tracking-and-integrations.md](references/forms-tracking-and-integrations.md).

## Build or hand off

If the user requests implementation:

1. Resolve the actual authorized site or browser capability.
2. Use the applicable site-building instructions and inspect the existing project before editing.
3. Preserve existing working integrations and unrelated user changes.
4. Implement the approved page, form, responsive states, thank-you state and tracking that are within scope.
5. Test the form without creating unauthorized external records; use safe test paths and clearly mark anything requiring the user's live credentials or approval.
6. Verify mobile and desktop output.
7. Report what is live, what was tested and what remains pending.

If the requested platform cannot be accessed, deliver a platform-specific build brief or local source artifact rather than refusing the entire task.

Use specialized installed skills for upstream facts or regulated channel requirements when they are more specific, but keep ownership of page architecture and final integration.

## Compliance and claims

- Separate law, platform policy, brokerage/company policy and best practice.
- Verify current jurisdictional requirements when material.
- Never state that a logo, license placement, disclaimer, schema type or form field is universally required without verification.
- For real estate, address Fair Housing, property-data authority, brokerage identity, rates/incentives, privacy and consent.
- For regulated entrepreneur categories, use substantiated claims and appropriate review.
- Do not proceed with a known violation merely because the user acknowledges risk.

## Output

For copy or brief modes, deliver:

1. Executive decision
2. Assumptions and pending inputs
3. Page strategy and funnel role
4. Alternatives and recommendation when applicable
5. Headline concepts
6. Wireframe and complete copy
7. Form and consent specification
8. Thank-you/delivery experience
9. Visual and responsive direction
10. Tracking and integration map
11. SEO/AEO specification only when selected
12. Test plan, KPIs and launch checklist
13. Sources
14. Quality score

For build mode, add the page link or artifact and verification results.

## Quality gate

Read [references/quality-and-testing.md](references/quality-and-testing.md). Do not deliver below 90/100 and correct all critical failures.
