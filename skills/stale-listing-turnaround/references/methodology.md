# Methodology notes

Use this reference when the case requires valuation, source reconciliation, carrying-cost analysis, or a seller-facing relaunch recommendation.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a cited source.
- **Derived metric:** calculated from verified inputs; show formula.
- **Inference:** interpretation supported by multiple observations.
- **Assumption:** user-editable placeholder used because evidence is absent.
- **Unknown:** material fact that cannot currently be established.

## Comparable roles

| Status | Primary use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Sold | Indication of market value | May need time, condition and concession adjustments |
| Active | Current substitution and seller competition | Asking price is not proof of value |
| Pending | Demand and likely market acceptance | Contract price and concessions may be unknown |
| Expired/withdrawn | Resistance and failed positioning | Withdrawal may be unrelated to price |

Build adjustments from paired sales, regression, grouped market evidence or another supportable method when data permits. If it does not, use qualitative bracketing and widen the range rather than inventing precise adjustments.

## Valuation reconciliation

Use current adjusted sales as the primary indication. Use appreciation from the prior sale only as a reasonableness check. Explain divergence through improvements, deterioration, micro-location, concessions, changing market composition and data quality.

## Funnel diagnostic

| Pattern | Strong hypotheses to test |
|---|---|
| Low views | Distribution, mapping, category, demand, portal data |
| High views / low showings | Price, cover photo, restrictions, buyer mismatch |
| Showings / no offers | Value gap, condition, layout, terms, location objection |
| Offers / no agreement | Negotiation, seller expectations, financing, contingencies |
| Contract / failed close | Inspection, appraisal, insurance, title, HOA, financing |

These are hypotheses, not automatic diagnoses. Confirm them with same-segment benchmarks and feedback.

## Financial distinctions

- Cash outflow answers: “What leaves the account each month?”
- Operating carry excludes mortgage principal.
- Economic carry adds an explicit opportunity-rate assumption to operating carry.
- Market risk belongs in a separate scenario.
- Net proceeds compare alternatives after payoff, liens, costs, credits and repairs.

Avoid counting tax and insurance twice when a mortgage payment includes escrow.

## Confidence rubric

- **High:** primary/current sources, strong comparability, consistent funnel evidence.
- **Medium:** adequate public data with some missing private inputs or mixed signals.
- **Low:** sparse comps, portal-only history, no feedback, or major unresolved facts.

State what evidence would upgrade or downgrade confidence.

## Seller communication

Translate technical evidence into a decision without overstating certainty. Prefer: “The current evidence indicates…” and “If this signal does not improve by the agreed trigger…” Avoid guarantees, shame, invented scarcity, or the claim that every day creates an identical loss.
