# Florida source map

Use the most specific primary source available. Verify current URLs during research.

## Source classes

- **Primary/official:** agency, legislature, local government, court filing, regulator, association dataset, authorized MLS report, company filing or direct announcement about the issuing organization.
- **Secondary journalism:** independently reported local, business or trade publication. Use for context, quotes and discovery; trace material rules and figures to primary documents when available.
- **Industry commentary:** expert analysis, brokerage research or vendor interpretation. Disclose methodology and incentives.
- **Social discovery:** public posts, videos, forums, comments and search snippets. Use to identify questions or narratives, never as sole verification of a material claim.

## State and local

- Florida Department of Financial Services, Office of Insurance Regulation and other applicable Florida agencies for insurance, licensing, commerce and emergency data.
- Florida Legislature and official bill/statute records for legal changes.
- County property appraisers, tax collectors, clerks, planning and building departments.
- City and county commission agendas, adopted plans, permits and infrastructure pages.

## Market

- Florida Realtors and local Realtor associations.
- Authorized MLS reports.
- Federal sources: Census, BLS, FRED, FHFA, FEMA and other applicable agencies.

## Development

- Official builder/developer pages and filings.
- Government permits, plans and public meeting documents.
- Employer press releases only for the employer's own announcement.

## Secondary context

Use reputable local journalism to explain context, not replace the primary source for a material figure or rule. Possible discovery/context sources include established local outlets, South Florida Business Journal, The Real Deal and Inman, subject to access and article-specific evaluation. These are not government or regulatory sources and must not be labeled official.

For paywalled or inaccessible reporting, do not infer the article's contents from a headline or snippet. Find an accessible primary source or state the access limitation.

## Multi-channel discovery

- Search public Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Reddit and other channels only when available and appropriate.
- Record the exact query/community, observation date, visible engagement signal and sampling limitation.
- Do not claim comprehensive coverage of a platform.
- Do not access private groups, bypass authentication/paywalls or reuse copyrighted creative.
- Validate each selected story independently of its social popularity.

## Source ledger

Record claim, source, URL, effective period, publication date, consulted date, geography, methodology, limitation and confidence.
