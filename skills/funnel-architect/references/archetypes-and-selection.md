## Selection rules

Select by buyer awareness, offer complexity, price, urgency, proof burden, traffic temperature, sales capacity and budget. Price alone never selects the funnel.

## Core archetypes

| Archetype | Best fit | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet | Cold audience with a research or education gap | More leads, slower qualification |
| Quiz or assessment | A problem that benefits from diagnosis or segmentation | Better data, more build complexity |
| Webinar or workshop | Complex offer requiring belief change | Strong education, attendance friction |
| Direct-to-call | High intent or high-value consultative sale | Faster path, lower cold conversion |
| Application-gated | Limited capacity or expensive sales time | Better qualification, lower volume |
| VSL or sales page | Repeatable offer with sufficient proof | Scalable, proof and copy intensive |
| Trial or demo | Product must be experienced | Product adoption becomes the bottleneck |
| Conversational/DM | Audience already interacts on social or messaging | High response, operator and platform dependence |
| Event | Date-specific commitment and community proof | Deadline advantage, operational load |
| Reactivation | Owned database with valid contact authority | Low media cost, consent/data quality risk |
| Referral/partner | Trust transfers through a known source | Slow partner development, strong lead quality |

## Real estate patterns

| Goal | Recommended starting pattern |
|---|---|
| First-time buyers | Local guide or affordability assessment → consultation |
| New construction | Community/property-list offer → qualification → appointment |
| Sellers | Equity/market report → consultation → listing appointment |
| Home valuation | Address capture → expectation setting → human valuation follow-up |
| Open house | RSVP → reminders → attendance segmentation → follow-up |
| Relocation | Relocation guide/quiz → needs assessment → consultation |
| Investor | Deal criteria/analysis offer → application → strategy call |
| Agent recruiting | Workshop/content → application or conversation |
| Database | Segment → relevant update → reply or appointment |

Avoid one funnel that promotes several unrelated audiences or offers. Use a routing page or separate funnels when intents differ materially.

## Combination rules

- Combine archetypes only when each stage has a distinct job.
- Do not insert a webinar, quiz or application because it appears sophisticated.
- Reduce steps for high-intent traffic.
- Add qualification before scarce sales capacity.
- Add education before a complex or skeptical decision.
- Add human contact where trust, nuance or regulation makes automation insufficient.
