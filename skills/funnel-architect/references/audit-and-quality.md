# Audit and Quality Score

Score 100 points:

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Diagnosis and evidence | 15 |
| Audience, offer and decision path | 15 |
| Architecture and sequencing | 15 |
| Unit economics and capacity | 15 |
| CRM, consent and handoff | 10 |
| Tracking and measurement | 10 |
| Trade-offs and recommendation | 10 |
| Risk, compliance and factual integrity | 10 |

Do not deliver below 90.

## Audit output

For each funnel layer provide:

- status;
- evidence;
- likely cause;
- business impact;
- confidence;
- recommended action;
- owner;
- KPI;
- priority.

Rank fixes using:

`priority = expected impact × confidence ÷ effort`

Use qualitative ranges when exact scoring would imply false precision.

## Launch gates

- offer and CTA approved;
- all material facts verified or visibly pending;
- form and consent tested;
- notifications and CRM routing tested;
- thank-you state works;
- tracking events verified;
- mobile experience checked;
- suppression and exit logic checked;
- owner and response SLA assigned;
- rollback or pause condition defined.
