# Research, Compliance, and Risk

## Research decision

Research when a fact is current, local, regulated, material to economics, or explicitly requested. Skip browsing for stable framework work or minor edits that do not depend on current facts.

Use primary sources for platform specifications, laws, official programs and product documentation. Use reputable market sources for benchmarks and label methodology and date.

## Claim ledger

Classify each material claim:

- **Verified:** supported by a cited source and date.
- **User-provided:** attributed to the user, not independently verified.
- **Assumption:** used for modeling and labeled.
- **Pending:** required before launch.
- **Prohibited:** deceptive, discriminatory, unsupported or outside authorized scope.

## Compliance layers

Separate:

1. law and regulation;
2. platform or carrier policy;
3. brokerage, employer or company policy;
4. professional review;
5. best practice.

Do not present a best practice as law. Do not proceed with a known violation because the user acknowledges risk.

## Real estate

When applicable, verify:

- Fair Housing and anti-steering;
- platform special-category restrictions;
- brokerage identity and advertising rules;
- permission to use listing, MLS, builder, image and testimonial data;
- rates, payments, incentives and availability dates;
- consent for email, SMS and calls;
- privacy policy and data handling;
- jurisdiction-specific disclosure requirements.

## Failure register

At minimum consider:

- weak offer;
- message-market mismatch;
- insufficient budget or data;
- slow lead response;
- CRM or notification failure;
- false conversion tracking;
- unqualified volume;
- operator capacity;
- claim or consent risk;
- stale inventory, pricing or event details;
- no clear experiment owner.
