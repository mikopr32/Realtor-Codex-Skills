# Funnel Architect

Design the operating system around the conversion, then coordinate its execution. Do not stop at a diagram or invent unavailable skills.

## Select the operating mode

Choose one:

1. **Strategy:** design a new funnel.
2. **Audit:** diagnose an existing funnel.
3. **Optimization:** find and correct the highest-leverage bottleneck.
4. **Build:** design the system and coordinate creation of its assets.
5. **Measurement:** interpret funnel data and specify the next experiment.

If the user requests a complete funnel, default to **Build**. Do not refuse because the request includes copy or implementation. Resolve and use available specialist capabilities; otherwise produce the missing asset or an execution-ready brief within the authorized scope.

## Establish context

1. Determine business track, audience, offer, geography, brand, language, jurisdiction, and requested outcome from the conversation and authorized project context.
2. Use personal context when prior user-specific decisions materially affect the result.
3. Never scan unrelated files broadly. Read only relevant user-provided or clearly named project resources.
4. Distinguish confirmed facts, user claims, working assumptions, unknowns, and time-sensitive facts.
5. Ask one grouped intake only when a missing decision would materially change architecture, economics, compliance, or implementation. Skip known fields.

Critical intake:

- conversion goal and deadline;
- audience and problem;
- offer, price, margin or transaction value;
- current assets, traffic, CRM and follow-up;
- volume goal and budget;
- language, market and jurisdiction;
- operational capacity and close process.

If exact numbers are unavailable, use labeled scenarios rather than fabricate a baseline.

## Diagnose before designing

Map the current system across:

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Market and offer | Is the promise relevant, differentiated and defensible? |
| Acquisition | Can the right audience discover the offer? |
| Capture | Is there a clear value exchange and consent path? |
| Nurture | Does follow-up move the lead toward a decision? |
| Conversion | Is the next action appropriate to intent and price? |
| Close | Are qualification, appointment, sales and handoff defined? |
| Delivery | Does fulfillment create the promised result? |
| Retention | Are onboarding, repeat business, referral and reactivation designed? |
| Data | Are source, stage, consent and outcomes captured? |
| Measurement | Can the operator locate the bottleneck? |

Use these states:

- ✅ Working with evidence
- ⚠️ Present but underperforming
- ❌ Missing
- ❓ Present but unmeasured
- ⛔ Evidence or access unavailable

For audits, show the diagnosis before recommendations and identify the three highest-leverage constraints.

## Compare viable architectures

When more than one route is reasonable, present two or three options with:

- speed to launch;
- cash requirement;
- operational load;
- scalability;
- data requirement;
- compliance risk;
- likely failure mode.

Use [references/archetypes-and-selection.md](references/archetypes-and-selection.md) to select or combine an archetype. Recommend one and explain why it dominates for the user's constraints.

## Lock the offer and decision path

Define:

- target segment and triggering problem;
- promised outcome and mechanism;
- deliverable or value exchange;
- proof available and claims allowed;
- risk reversal when genuine;
- one primary conversion event;
- qualification requirements;
- next logical offer after conversion.

Do not manufacture stacked values, guarantees, scarcity, urgency, testimonials, rates, savings, performance or outcomes.

## Model funnel economics

Read [references/unit-economics-and-measurement.md](references/unit-economics-and-measurement.md).

Work backward from the business goal:

`sales target → qualified appointments → booked appointments → leads → visits/impressions → budget`

Show formulas, assumptions, low/base/high scenarios, and operational capacity. Calculate CPL, cost per appointment and CAC ceilings only from verified economics or clearly labeled inputs. If the funnel cannot work under plausible economics, say so and redesign it.

## Design the complete system

Specify:

1. traffic sources and audience temperature;
2. offer and message by stage;
3. capture experience and consent;
4. nurture and branching;
5. conversion event;
6. sales and human handoff;
7. fulfillment, retention and referral;
8. CRM fields, stages, tags and ownership;
9. tracking, attribution and dashboard;
10. build order, dependencies and launch gates.

Include one minimum viable funnel and the scaled version. Favor the smallest system that can prove the core hypothesis.

## Resolve capabilities and execute

Read [references/handoff-and-build-contracts.md](references/handoff-and-build-contracts.md).

1. Inspect the capabilities actually available in the current environment.
2. Prefer the most specific installed skill or authorized tool.
3. Never name or promise a skill that has not been resolved.
4. Pass a structured brief containing facts, assumptions, objective, audience, offer, language, CTA, proof, compliance constraints, dependencies, KPIs and acceptance criteria.
5. Validate each returned asset against the shared brief before moving downstream.
6. If the user asked only for strategy, stop after the implementation plan.
7. If the user asked to build, continue through all reversible in-scope assets. Pause only for a material user decision, missing authorization, connector authentication, or an external action outside scope.

Do not publish, activate spend, send messages, submit forms, or alter live systems unless the user explicitly authorizes that action and the required connector is available.

## Research and compliance

Read [references/research-compliance-and-risk.md](references/research-compliance-and-risk.md) whenever the design depends on current benchmarks, platform rules, legal requirements, financial claims, regulated industries or local market facts.

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Date time-sensitive facts.
- Treat benchmarks as ranges and hypotheses.
- Separate law, platform policy, brokerage/company policy and best practice.
- Build a safe alternative instead of proceeding with a known violation.

For real estate, address Fair Housing, advertising and brokerage requirements, property-data authorization, consent, privacy and platform special-category rules when applicable. Verify current jurisdiction and platform rules rather than hard-coding universal requirements.

## Delivery format

Deliver:

1. **Executive decision**
2. **Assumptions and unknowns**
3. **Current-state diagnosis**
4. **Options and trade-offs**
5. **Recommended funnel map**
6. **Offer and message architecture**
7. **Unit economics and capacity**
8. **Asset and infrastructure build order**
9. **Capability execution queue**
10. **Measurement and experiment plan**
11. **Risks and failure scenarios**
12. **30/60/90 rollout** when the scope is a business system or the user invokes AMI mode

Use a compact table or diagram only when it improves comprehension.

## Quality gate

Read [references/audit-and-quality.md](references/audit-and-quality.md). Do not deliver below 90/100. Correct critical failures regardless of score.

Critical failures:

- designing before diagnosing;
- no primary conversion event;
- fabricated data or proof;
- economics disconnected from the goal;
- no consent, tracking or exit logic;
- invented handoff capability;
- recommendation without trade-offs;
- current regulatory or platform claim left unverified when material.
